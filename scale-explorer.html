Baby Scale Explorer

Connects to a Bluetooth LE scale from Chrome, lists every service and characteristic, subscribes to notifications and logs raw bytes. Built for the Greater Goods Smart Baby Scale (shows up as something like BS1711-B), but works on any BLE device.

How to capture

  1. Close the GG app on your phone and turn the phone's Bluetooth off. Most of these scales accept only one connection, and the phone will grab it first.
  2. Wake the scale (press a button or put something on it). The Bluetooth icon on its display should blink.
  3. Click Pick device and connect, choose the scale in Chrome's list (try a blank name filter if nothing shows), and wait for the GATT table.
  4. Put a known weight on the scale, enter it above, and press HOLD. Watch the log for notifications. Then try the scale's unit button and watch which bits flip.
  5. If nothing arrives, click Read all readable values, then try Watch advertisements (needs chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features turned on).
  6. Save the log as JSON and send it back. That file is enough to write the decoder.
